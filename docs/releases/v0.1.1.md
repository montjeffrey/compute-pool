A packaging release: no changes to how **pool** behaves.

## What's new

- **Windows version details.** `pool.exe` now shows its product name, version and copyright under *Properties → Details*.
- **Signed release tag.** The `v0.1.1` tag, and the commits it covers from now on, are signed and show as **Verified** on GitHub.

## Install

1. Download the binary for your platform below, and `SHA256SUMS`.
2. Verify it:
   - Linux/macOS: `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing`
   - Windows: `Get-FileHash pool-0.1.1-windows-amd64.exe` and compare.
3. Rename it to `pool` (or `pool.exe`) and put it on your `PATH`.

See the [v0.1.0 notes](https://github.com/montjeffrey/compute-pool/releases/tag/v0.1.0) for what pool does, and [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/montjeffrey/compute-pool/blob/v0.1.1/SECURITY.md) before you run an agent.

## Known limits

- **Binaries are not code-signed yet**, so Windows may still show "Unknown publisher" or a SmartScreen warning. On macOS, clear the quarantine flag with `xattr -d com.apple.quarantine pool`.
